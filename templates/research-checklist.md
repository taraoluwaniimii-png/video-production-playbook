# Research Checklist

Research isn't done when I'm tired of it. It's done when every box below is honestly checked.

- [ ] I can state the video's core claim in one sentence.
- [ ] I found at least one thing that genuinely surprised me.
- [ ] I can name the strongest counter-argument — and my answer to it.
- [ ] I have 2–3 citable facts with real sources (not just "I read somewhere").
- [ ] I know whose human story carries the idea (a person, a classroom, a case).
- [ ] I checked whether my angle has been done to death — and mine is different.
- [ ] I noted what I *couldn't* verify, so it doesn't end up in the script.
- [ ] The production brief's research questions are all answered.

Rule: no source, no claim. If it can't be sourced, it gets cut or flagged as opinion — on screen, not just in my head.
