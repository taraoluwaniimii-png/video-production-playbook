# Publishing Checklist

Nothing goes live until every box is checked.

## Content
- [ ] Script read aloud once — no awkward sentences survived.
- [ ] Every on-screen claim traces back to research notes.
- [ ] Hook delivers what the title/thumbnail promise.
- [ ] Captions/subtitles added and spot-checked.

## Audio
- [ ] Voiceover pace feels natural (not rushed, not dragging).
- [ ] Royalty-free music mixed low under voiceover — never competing.
- [ ] Sound effects tasteful, not decorative noise.

## Visuals
- [ ] Every beat has its visual — no dead air over a static frame.
- [ ] Text on screen is readable on a phone (big, short, high contrast).

## Packaging
- [ ] Title written for humans first, search second.
- [ ] Description includes the core claim + relevant links.
- [ ] Hashtags/tags chosen per platform (YouTube / Instagram / TikTok).
- [ ] Thumbnail concept matches the hook — no clickbait the video can't cash.

## After publishing
- [ ] Pinned comment or reply plan for the first wave of comments.
- [ ] Noted one thing to improve next time (the playbook is a living document).
